Run the `blueprint-validate` skill and follow its steps exactly.

Find the Blueprint, ask who is validating and whether they have the authority to move a rating,
then walk the ratings one at a time ordered by consequence, pressure-test the exception paths, and
work the open questions that gate Automate-rated steps. Record confirmations as well as changes,
and never write a Status that claims more than the session covered.

The operator who gave the capture interview cannot validate their own Blueprint, and neither can
you. If no risk owner is available, say so and record the session as input gathering that does not
clear the automation gate.
